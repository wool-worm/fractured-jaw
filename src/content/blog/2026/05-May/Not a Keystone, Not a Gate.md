Here we go, writing a first post, this is a rite of passage in the blog world, or so I’ve heard. While it doesn’t have to be anything groundbreaking, it actually seems to be quite hard to compose. If you could see all the ways I’ve delayed writing this, all the late late nights spent developing new features for the site, scrolling hundreds of pages of fonts just to find the perfect ones, its borderline comedic. I’ve literally worked to exhaustion to avoid writing this post, the exact point of the site itself; there is some wonderful irony in that.

I think there is a certain pressure to say something that will summarize the whole raison d'être of this project, like dropping a keystone in an archway, something that will support the whole thing through from beginning to conclusion. There is an assumption there that I know where this will all go and all there is to do is to chisel the right shape and drop it into place.

In reality, I have no idea what the shape of this thing is or will be, I think the obscurity itself is what drew me to the project from the start. Why bother doing a thing if you know exactly what form it will take, the path it will be drawn along? The obscurity and unknown quality of even just this small corner of the internet is what I’ve been craving and what I sense many people are craving as well.

__> Time for a poetic tangent, buffering…__

This experience of mystery mediates the relationship between ourselves and the world in truly fundamental ways. Often times, it can feel, to me at least, like stepping through a gateway that reveals some previously unknown, fine-grained structure to what a moment before did not possess it. It seems to be both experience, in the sense that it places you in a certain wild dimension, and not experience, in the sense that it is the antithesis of knowledge and the eradication of all previous assumptions. In those ways, the unknown, the alien, and the foreign are themselves also a gate and simultaneously not a gate.

__> Stream ended, cache cleared__

I think I just caught myself writing about the site, why I made it, and what (I hope) it is for. Now, do I think that *all of that* is going to actually materialize here on this tiny blog, no. But in a different and truer sense, yes. In a tiny way maybe we can contribute here to a growing refutation of the all-encompassing, all-consuming certainty that circumscribes our experience and defines our times. I wrote about this briefly on the [[pages/about|about page]], but I’ll restate it: hopefully anyone contributing here (myself included) can, in some way, reintroduce anyone drifting in from the net to that wilder dimension where the other and the alien live, as this is also where human connection lives. That’s pretty lofty for a little blog site, so I’ll add that you can expect some inane and irreverent bullshit too, stay tuned.

So hello world! Nice to meet you, hope you find something weird and wonderful.
