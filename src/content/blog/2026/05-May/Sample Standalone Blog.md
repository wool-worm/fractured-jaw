A blog post that isn't a field note. The thing that makes a post a blog rather than an essay, around here, is mostly its temperature: blog posts are written hot, essays are written cold. This is a blog post. The hand is still moving.

When two names sit on a byline, the typical convention is the first did the heavy lifting and the second came in late with an edit. That's not the convention here. The order is alphabetical, which means nothing about who did what.
