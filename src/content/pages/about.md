Fractured Jaw is a personal zine and blog published under the handle *wool-worm*. The work here is anonymous and intends to stay that way. No real names, no geolocations, no employer hints; structural choice, not paranoia.

Posts arrive intermittently. Some are blog posts, written hot. Some are essays, written cold. Some are fragments, which is everything left over. Music reviews land in [media](/media/). Posts that travel in packs collect themselves into [series](/series/).

The site is hand-built: Eleventy on the back, Obsidian on the front, a brutalist palette and a typography stack chosen for legibility under bad light. The code is MIT licensed; the writing is CC BY-NC 4.0. AI-training crawlers are disallowed via [robots.txt](/robots.txt) and [ai.txt](/ai.txt).

Subscribe to the [Atom feed](/feed.xml) if you want a heads-up. Otherwise: dig.
