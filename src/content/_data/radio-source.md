Communication reaches its maximum velocity when like responds to like, when a chain reaction of likeness occurs. The negativity of alterity and foreignness, in other words the resistance of the other, disturbs and delays the smooth communication of the same. Transparency stabilizes and speeds the system by eliminating the other and the alien. This systemic compulsion makes the society of transparency a calibrated society.

---

The internet of things represents the completion of the transparency society. The transparency society has become indistinguishable from a society of total surveillance. Everything around us observes and surveys us, tracking what we do and do not do. Today, every click of the mouse is recorded. Every step we take can be reconstructed. We leave digital traces everywhere. Our digital habitus is completely captured online. The total recording of life is will replace all trust with information and control. Trust makes it possible to entertain relations with other people in the absence of complete knowledge about them. Because digital networks make it so easy to obtain information, trust becomes less and less important as a social practice. It gives way to control. 

___

In all realms we are increasingly compelled towards an addiction to immediacy. This addiction requires nothing less than our complete and infinitely divided attention, and demands nothing short of absolute smoothness and transparency in its search for maximum efficiency. In immediacy, there can be no lingering, no peering into, no surprises. In this condition, we must abolish all sense of the alien. In this addiction we have become increasingly self-obsessed and afflicted with a certain totalitarian mindstate, one in which all external experience must be a servitor to our own need for clearness and surety that nothing unexpected will ever happen.
