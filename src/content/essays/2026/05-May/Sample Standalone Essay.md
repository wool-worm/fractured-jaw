![[_attachments/essays/notes-on-a-machine-listening-practice/image.png|Monastery|500]]

Essays here are the cold-written counterpart to the blog. The same material would land as a blog post if I wrote it the same day I had the thought, and as an essay if I let it sit a week. Sometimes the version that survives the wait is barely related to the original spark.

What this format buys you, structurally, is room. Blogs run a few hundred words. Fragments run shorter. Essays can sprawl when sprawl is the right shape, and the layout here doesn't fight it.
