Let it be known that I'm a big fan of the month of May. Sure, sometimes it's kinda wet and rainy, but it's also filled with lots of that nice shoulder season weather before summer rolls in and rips you a new one for three months straight. So you got to really get out there and enjoy it. But when you're not 'out there' but 'in here' (that chronically online void-dwelling lifestyle - I get it, but do try to get out some), you should at least be enjoying yourself with some high quality tunes.

Now, I'm no critic, but I've certainly got an eclectic taste and spend way more time than a normal person does listening to and searching for new music, so it's my duty to share that with anyone who is willing to listen. All that said, let's talk a bit about some of the most interesting music released in May for me. I'll keep it to just my top five since all of our minds are too rattled by truly terrible short-form content to really chew on much more than that at this point.

## Ebende - Dream City
### Favorite Track: *Detour*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/ebende/dream-city/album]]", "compact" %}

This is a short lil' album that really kicked off the month for me. Packed full of moody breakbeats and ambient drone-y sketches, I would not recommend you skipping this one as I enjoyed it *All The Way Through*. Yeah, you're right, that's one of the tracks title’s, but I'm allowed to make jokes like that. Oh, and he actually released another album this month as well, the other being [Transmission](https://open.spotify.com/album/3CPv74bjTal2iByNfDhr6q). It was also great, I just like this one better. So check them both out.

---

## Desecresy - The Secret of Death 
### Favorite Track: *Gorge of the Dead*

{% bandcamp "[[_data/media/music/desecresy/the-secret-of-death/album]]" %}

Every time this dude—yeah, it's just one dude from Poland making this stuff—drops an album and I check out his page, I'm just shocked he has such few listeners. I love it, just incredibly heavy and dense doom metal (I apologize in advance for mis-categorizing any subgenres, I cannot be held responsible for that as there's just too many at this point and I can't keep up or be bothered). It's so heavy, just relentlessly heavy. Metal vocals can be hit or miss for me, but the deep growling here is a hit for me all day. I'd say this has gotten the most playthroughs for me this month by far.

---

## Lake Haze - VHS Memories 
### Favorite Track: *Love Mnemonics*

{% bandcamp "[[_data/media/music/lake-haze/vhs-memories-lp/album]]" %}

I can't help but just being so drawn to lo-fi house music. Maybe it's the hazy nostalgic feeling associated with the 90's or maybe it's just the irresistible four-on-the-floor grooves, but I know that I love it. This Lake Haze album did not let me down and I'm pretty sure it will be there for you also if you're into this vibe like I am.

---

## Namasenda - Limbo
### Favorite Track: *Coquette*

{% bandcamp "[[_data/media/music/namasenda/limbo/album]]" %}

I am unashamedly a massive hyperpop fan so I was pumped to see this album come across my feed. She's worked with some big names in the space - A.G. Cook, Danny L. Harle, Hannah Diamond - but, for me, this album really solidified her as someone I'm going to be paying attention to more moving forward. Lots of synth-y grooves in here that work really well with her vocals. It's just such a catchy album.

---

## Active Child - Active Child
### Favorite Track: *Coming Up*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/active-child/active-child/album]]", "compact" %}

So you would think this is his first album, seeing as it's self-titled and all, but it's not. I'm so ancient that I remember being majorly into his actual first album back in 2011, [You Are All I See](https://open.spotify.com/album/5CCPRE1uOQzYAnOkxxwz2f), which I recommend you also check out while you're at it. But since I'm just talking about timely, relevant releases here, this latest album is just great. It's kind of like easy listening rock made by a dude with a beautiful falsetto going on. Well, it's not "kind of like" that, that's really just what it is. Do give it a spin if you are so inclined.
