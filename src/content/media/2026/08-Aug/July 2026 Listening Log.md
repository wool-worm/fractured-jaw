## Ken Carson - xperiment
### Favorite Track: *shopping (with Destroy Lonely)*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/ken-carson/xperiment/album]]", "compact" %}

I’ve been listening to a lot more “rage” (weak genre name in my opinion, it’s far too reductive) music lately. For me, it’s all about the production, as some of the lyricism is pretty mid and not that clever, but I totally dig the blown-out speakers sound. Like scratches my eardrums or something. But I will say that this has the best flow from Ken that I have heard so that’s exciting. Shows some character development, I appreciate it.

## Bring Me The Horizon - Count Your Blessings | Repented
### Favorite Track: *Dehumanized*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/bring-me-the-horizon/count-your-blessings-repented/album]]", "compact" %}

This was a real treat for aging millennials who really cut their teeth on the 2000s deathcore. I had no idea this was even happening but had a buddy send me a link for the one new track on here, Dehumanized, and it blew me away. Dudes are like 40 and still can make music like it’s 2006. Apparently they weren’t happy with the original recording so they literally recreated the whole thing - which is incredible. Better quality and still hits just as hard as it did 20 years ago, I’ll take it.

## Hello Meteor - The Oahu GP: World Tour
### Favorite Track: *CONGRATULATIONS!: WINNER’S CIRCLE*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/hello-meteor/the-oahu-gp-world-tour/album]]", "compact" %}

I straight up love all of Hello Meteor’s stuff, definitely a go-to station of mine, but I love their Oahu GP motorsport video game themed albums the best (*The Oahu* *GP 2: Hyper Tropic* is the goat). No exception here, this nails the tropical racing vibe throughout. Could soundtrack a remake of Cruis’n USA no problem, would sound incredibly authentic. Definitely spiritually adjacent.

## Nueen - Swerved
### Favorite Track: *Searchtiers*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/nueen/swerved/album]]", "compact" %}

I loved his album with Defled earlier this year (*xnd*, go check it out) so I was pumped for this one. It didn't disappoint either - super moody, super atmospheric, exactly what I've come to expect from his content. Solid choice of features with Xzxavier Stone and Space Afrika (and another Defled track, ahh yeahh), too. Big fan.

### Sangam - Dynasty
### Favorite Track: *Lost Brothers*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/sangam/dynasty/album]]", "compact" %}

Anyone that knows me knows that my truest music love is for Sangam. I've loved every album he has put out, have been straight up on it since 2016 (and my Spotify Wrapped almost always has him as my top artist). *Dynasty* is no different - even though it sounds so similar to his other stuff, I know, I know - and I loved it. It actually came out hot on the tail of his previous album from June, *Elusive Path*, which I totally missed until a week into this July. Shameful.
