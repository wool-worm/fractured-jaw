## Gloomcvlt - ONE THAT CAN SEE
### Favorite Track: *NIGHTS ALL MINE*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/gloomcvlt/one-that-can-see/album]]", "compact" %}

In one of those situations where the artist’s name pretty much spot on reflects the sound they are achieving, Gloomcvlt really made some moody, gloomy, culty tunes here. Throbbing, smoky nightclub feeling. Technically in the vaporwave space, but I feel that’s an oversimplification for what’s going on in this album. It’s got a major groove to it.

## not even noticed - space beyond noise
### Favorite Track: *laguna*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/not-even-noticed/space-beyond-noise/album]]", "compact" %}

Growing up in the 90s, lo-fi techno (“classic” techno, I guess) is something that has always spoken to me. Just the whole idea of city warehouse ambience makes me nostalgic even though I was a literal child at the time. Not sure how that works if I’m being honest, but this album really plugged into that for me. Huge fan.

## Da Vosk Docta - IMMACULATE CONSTELLATION
### Favorite Track: *Vagary Park (Unearthed Memory)*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/da-vosk-docta/immaculate-constellation/album]]", "compact" %}

I think this guy comes from the mid-2010s Russian hard wave scene, but he is still killing it nowadays. Actually better than a lot of his earlier stuff, but still give that a listen after this one. Really vibe-y stuff all around.

## Earth Trax - Everlasting Flame
### Favorite Track: *Towboat*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/earth-trax/everlasting-flame/album]]", "compact" %}

This was the album that got me through all the rainy days and work travel this past month, truly. Lo-fi techno at its core, this one had a lot more ‘feelings’ I guess than some of Earth Trax’s older stuff. Not like it’s any better necessarily, it just felt different - and I liked that. It’s always refreshing to me when an artist with a refined, recognizable sound just slightly pivots. Far from a complete reinvention, but enough to make the latest album feel markedly different. That’s going on here, I highly recommend putting this one on as background focus music or something like that.

## Soudiere & Roland Jones - Game Is Tight III
### Favorite Track - *True 2 This*

{% spotify "[[_data/media/music/soudiere/game-is-tight-3/album]]", "compact" %}

I’m a huge fan of both of these artists when they are making solo stuff so I’m majorly sold on anything they are doing together. This is the third installment of their “Game Is Tight” collaboration, hopefully a long-running thing they do with any luck. It’s got the classic Memphis triplets sampling and incredible bass throughout, highly recommend checking this one out and all their albums before it while you’re at it.
