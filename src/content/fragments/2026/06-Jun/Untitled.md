Stand with me
Sigh
Let the sea caress our feet
Let leviathans lay
Staid by waves in restful slumber

Dive with me
Breathe
The gasp before the plunge
Toward streaming kelp like heralds
And shipwrecked ramparts, guarded

Feel with me
Release
Driftwood fortress, set aflame
Coral bastion, struck asunder
Searching onward, ever deeper

Seek with me
Know:
That we cannot drown in these waters,
We need not pass that gate alone,
Through churning tides, twinned hearts in time
Rich souls our gleaming fortune.
