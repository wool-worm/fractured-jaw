So I finally got the scanner back from repair today, but the saga of the mystery stations continues. Some of you may know from my previous posts (re: [[fragments/2026/06-Jun/scanner signal not making sense, at a loss|scanner signal not making sense, at a loss]]) I’ve been having an awful time with this unit since patch_9981.A199 hit (my fault anyways for scraping it off a hypernet cracking site) and its only gotten worse. Cherti from Delta Block has put his eyes on every component and either he won’t admit he can’t figure it out or that he fucked it up somehow. Either way, he’s not gonna get it back, I’m figuring this out on my own—well, maybe with a little help. If anyone else in Gamma has experience with 5th gen scanners and has the patience of a saint, I could use the help (plus there might be a few creds in the deal too).

Anyways, I wanted to log this trawl sesh because we got some really interesting stuff washing up today! First off…

Tons and tons of organic signals today. I seriously haven’t seen this many floating around since those punks out in Delta raided the freqs and flooded the all four arco bands with their militant shit. I swear the next time they pull something like that, good ol’ LMMC isn’t gonna stop with the goons they sent in last time. There hasn’t been a justiciar in the arco for 20 years but that doesn’t mean they aren’t itching to send one in.

Right, I was gonna go over the logs.

First off I found a sweet little signal bobbing around `alpha0x06`. Pretty good quality for such a low freq but maybe they’ve got better software over in alpha (maybe someone should tell Cherti). This transmission has all the hallmarks of a hit: wicked breakbeats, cold brutal pads underline reverb drenched synth stabs. Its not all about the groove though, this one has got the atmosphere too.

Another good find on `gamma0x0E`, just like last week! This time they’re broadcasting some wild deconstructed ambient. Synth horns blinking in an out of racing arpeggiators, texture , texture, texture. Our very own Gamma dweller [[authors/boogra-boy|boogra-boy]] caught this one too, check out his other finds on the [[media/2026/06-Jun/May 2026 Listening Log|monthly roundup]]. Gotta give it a listen, sounds like nothing this side of the gatewall.

Not sure whats going on at `beta0x01`, I like it but not gonna lie to you, its weird. I had to check that my scanner wasn’t hitting one of those dead patches when I found this one, listen, you’ll know what I mean. Decaying tape hiss, muffled breathing, pops, clicks, whirrs like an air exchanger, and what got me, that haunted spoken word overdub. Which brings me to my last find of the day.

This one was early in the alpha freq, at `alpha0x02`, I actually trawled right past it before I hit `alpha0x06`. This is another example of what I’m officially dubbing “haunted stations.” I have the authority to do this because I’m pretty sure I’m the only one picking them up—either I’m going nuts (totally possible), my scanner is cracked (Cherti’s fault for sure), or I’m scooping something real. This one seems like an old defunct LMMC broadcast.

The thing that worries me is that it wasn’t there before. I swear, I may be incapable of making it to mess on time, but you might be surprised to learn that I keep meticulous logs. This was __not there__ last week. Dead air for miles and boom, out of nowhere this bugged out station? The thing is, it doesn’t seem to be a recording. If you don’t believe me, go check it out for yourself if you can. For the time being, I’m gonna assume these are real and I’ll try to find some more this week to report on.

This quick update turned into quite a post so this is me ending it. Scanners locked. Over.

-S
