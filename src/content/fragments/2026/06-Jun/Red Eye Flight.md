Back in the sky,
The wrong side of midnight.
Another month,
Year, no, lifetime passes by
The oval window,
Portal to an inky heaven.

Cumulus, nimbus,
Stratospheric, shrouded
in dark night's embrace.
These clouds a quilt,
For the slumbering Earth
On a Moonless night.

But in this plane,
Above those clouds,
SIeep has not found me yet.
Through this window,
Night's starless visage,
No door to any savior.

But in its stead
another face,
also starless,
ever darkened.
No portal to a mystic vision
But me upon a jet black mirror.
