"a hawk" she said,
pointing to a crow
carefree it soared
wheeling in the sky
a life lived divided
forced to walk or fly
now freed from her shackles to the Earth

"that hawk is me
and I am you"
sung softly by the wind
and seated still we gazed aloft
then turned our gaze within
